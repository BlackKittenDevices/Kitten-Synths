Kitten Synths

Kitten Synths — custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

A groovebox you play live: five tracks — three synths, a light synth and a drum machine — 13 synth engines (with six-operator FM, DX7 patches and Muug, a Minimoog-style engine with 100 sounds and its own editor), 250 sounds, 37 drum kits from 808 to techno, amapiano and ambient, your own samples and drum kits, 27 punch-in effects. 64 steps a pattern, four song slots, MIDI as SLOOP. Halloween black cats optional.
